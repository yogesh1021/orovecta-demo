METALWORKING SOLUTIONS

Designed Around Your Manufacturing Process
Metalworking performance depends on the interaction between material, tooling, machine, process and fluid. Orovecta provides solutions across different stages of metal processing.

CLEANERS

Clean surfaces. Prepare for the next process.

Solutions for removing:

Oil, grease, dirt, metal fines and process residues.

Applications
Component cleaning, inter-process cleaning and surface preparation.

RUST PREVENTIVE OILS

Protect components between processes.

Corrosion protection during:

Inter-process storage, transportation and finished component storage.

Purpose
Protection of ferrous components against atmospheric corrosion and flash rust.

FORMING OILS

Lubrication for demanding forming operations.

Suitable for:

Stamping, pressing, drawing, deep drawing, bending and sheet metal forming.

Benefits
Supports lubrication, surface protection and process performance.

SEMI-SYNTHETIC CUTTING FLUIDS

Cooling and lubrication for modern machining.

Suitable for:

Turning, milling, drilling, tapping, sawing and CNC machining.

Designed to support
Tool life, surface finish, cooling, lubrication and process stability.
