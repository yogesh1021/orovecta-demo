REEL TO REEL SELECTIVE PLATING

VR Industries has developed several cost-effective electroplating processes to ensure that every customer receives the quality finish they expect from an industry leader.

Reel to Reel Selective Plating : Reel-to-reel selective plating is a specific electroplating process where metal is selectively deposited onto specific areas of a continuous strip or reel of material. This process is commonly used in industries such as electronics, telecommunications, and automotive, where precision plating on specific areas is essential.
