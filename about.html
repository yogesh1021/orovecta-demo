Copper is a reddish pink coating that may be deposited as an undercoat or final plate.
Copper is a good barrier finish used to secure adhesion between a substrate and the next layer of plating by preventing attack on the substrate and providing a consistency pure interface. Copper plating has exceptional ‘levelling’ power that helps to smooth out or hide surface imperfections and to reduce polishing costs. Electroless Nickel is a hard Nickel phosphorus alloy of uniform thickness.
That means the EN coating thickness on the internal corner of the box shaped object can be the same as the coating thickness found on the external surfaces or sharp points. This coating has very high corrosion resistance and low porosity. The hardness of EN can be increased by heat treatment. EN is deposited without the use of electrical current but by a mechanism called autocatalysis in which Nickel is the catalyst. The abrasion resistance of EN is superior to that of electrolytic Nickel. EN is smooth and reduces galling tendency.

Services

VR INDUSTRIES has developed several cost-effective electroplating processes to ensure that every customer receives the quality finish they expect from an industry leader.

Reel to Reel Plating

Reel plating is the surface coating of flat strips, stamped or preformed electrical contacts attached to a carrier strip in continuous lengths.

Rack Plating

In Rack plating job parts are ‘hung’ on a metal rack (fixture) and get transported through the different stages of pre-treatment, electroplating and post-treatment.

Barrel Plating

Barrel plating is a mass finishing operation whereby the job parts are placed in a slow rotating barrel immersed in an electroplating bath.

Reel to Reel Selective Plating

Reel-to-reel selective plating is a specific electroplating process where metal is selectively deposited onto specific areas of a continuous strip or reel of material. This process is commonly used in industries such as electronics, telecommunications, and automotive, where precision plating on specific areas is essential.
