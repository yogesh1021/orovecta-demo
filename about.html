About Us

Industrial Lubrication. Engineered for Performance.

German Technology | Metalworking Fluids | Specialty Lubricants | MRO Solutions

Orovecta is an industrial lubrication brand with over 8 years of experience, providing technology-driven lubrication solutions for modern manufacturing and industrial applications.
Copper is a good barrier finish used to secure adhesion between a substrate and the next layer of plating by preventing attack on the substrate and providing a consistency pure interface. Copper plating has exceptional ‘levelling’ power that helps to smooth out or hide surface imperfections and to reduce polishing costs. Electroless Nickel is a hard Nickel phosphorus alloy of uniform thickness.
Our portfolio brings together Metalworking Fluids, Specialty Lubricants and MRO Lubrication Solutions, supported by application expertise and the capability to develop customized solutions for specific customer requirements. From metal preparation and machining to critical equipment lubrication, Orovecta focuses on delivering the right solution for the right application.
